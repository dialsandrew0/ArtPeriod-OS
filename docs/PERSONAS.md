# Personas

## Primary: Field Collector / Dealer

Finds works at estates, auctions, and secondary markets. Needs rapid, trustworthy triage rather than polished blue-chip tools.

## Secondary: Mid-market Collector

Wants collection tracking and realistic signals without enterprise pricing or false precision.

## Tertiary: Research / Appraisal Support

Needs a grounded, citable research index.

Design the field experience for the primary persona first.
